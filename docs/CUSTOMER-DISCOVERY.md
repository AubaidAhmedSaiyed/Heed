# Customer Discovery Strategy

HEED needs real external validation. We are targeting:
- Teams building autonomous agents
- DevOps automation teams
- Support automation teams

## Key Discovery Questions

1. What actions do your agents currently perform?
2. Which actions do you consider risky or irreversible?
3. Which systems do your agents currently access?
4. How are permissions and credentials currently managed for these agents?
5. What happens today when an agent behaves unexpectedly or drifts from its objective?
6. Can you currently pause an agent's execution mid-flow to ask for human approval?
7. Do you have action-level auditability for everything the agent touched?
8. Would you be willing to place a runtime control plane between your agents and external tools?
9. What concerns (latency, complexity, security) would prevent adoption of HEED?
10. What specific capabilities would you pay for in an enterprise version?
